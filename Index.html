Poultry Farming Tips

Brooding

Keep chicks warm at 32-35°C for the first week. Reduce temperature by 3°C weekly. Provide clean water and starter feed.

Feeding

Use quality feeds: chick mash, grower mash, and layer mash. Always provide grit and clean water. Avoid moldy feed.

Biosecurity

Clean and disinfect coops regularly. Isolate sick birds. Vaccinate on schedule. Control visitors and rodents.
